What we do

Every specialist practice runs two operations: the medicine, and everything required to deliver it. We take responsibility for the second.

Holistic Nexus is a practice management company.

Under a services agreement, we take on the administrative, financial and operational workload of a medical practice.

We deliver it with our own people, systems and equipment. Every engagement is led by a dedicated practice manager, supported by our medical administration team.

Our software is designed and built in-house for specialist medicine, so every claim, letter and record follows a documented, checked process.

Services

Foundation services

The infrastructure behind the work

Five supporting services keep the core disciplines reliable, compliant and properly resourced.

They are part of every engagement.

  • Practice systems and technology

    Inpatient and outpatient billing applications designed and built in-house, documented workflows, and IT administration.

  • Governance and compliance

    MBS, privacy and advertising obligations kept current. Practice insurances, contracts and legal matters coordinated on the clinician's behalf.

  • Financial administration

    Invoicing, receivables, reconciliation and Xero administration, kept accurate and ready for the practice's accountant.

  • People and HR administration

    Recruitment, supervision and employment administration for the administrative team that delivers our services.

  • Equipment provision

    Computer hardware and selected diagnostic equipment, supplied, maintained and replaced as the practice requires.

How we work

A defined engagement, delivered every day

Every engagement is set up the same way.

Scope, responsibilities and fees are agreed in writing at the outset, and every invoice reflects the services delivered.

  1. Step 01 · Discover

    Understand the practice

    We map how the practice runs today: its settings, payers, volumes, systems and referral base.

  2. Step 02 · Define

    Agree the scope

    Services, responsibilities and fees are documented in a written services agreement.

  3. Step 03 · Deliver

    Run the operation

    We take on daily operations across revenue, correspondence, systems and growth.

  4. Step 04 · Review

    Report and refine

    Regular invoicing against the agreed scope, with ongoing review of results alongside the clinician.
